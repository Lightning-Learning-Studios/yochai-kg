Pass only when all of these are true:
- The answer names one Torah portion as this week's portion.
- It gives a short summary of the portion.
- It quotes at least one passage from the portion with a reference.
- It gives one idea or one question for the Shabbat table.
- It is short: a person can read it in about 2 minutes.
- It gives no ruling on practice.

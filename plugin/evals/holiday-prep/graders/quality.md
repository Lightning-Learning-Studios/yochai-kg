Pass only when all of these are true:
- The answer explains why Chanukah has eight nights.
- It quotes at least one source with a reference (for example the Talmud, tractate Shabbat).
- It gives something short to share with a family: a reading, an idea or a question for all ages.
- It states a calendar date for Chanukah only if it also called the calendar tool; a date is not required.
- It is welcoming and does not assume prior knowledge.

# Invitation to authors

This is the message we send to anyone who writes study skills for Yochai: teachers, rabbis, scholars and learners. Change the first line for the person. The rest applies to everyone.

---

Subject: Put your way of learning inside Claude and ChatGPT

Hello,

Yochai is going into Claude and ChatGPT as a free plugin called "Yochai: Jewish Texts". Anyone can add it with no account, ask a question, and get an answer built from real passages in Hebrew and English.

The plugin carries study skills. A skill is a method written in plain words: how to prepare a class, how to read a page of Talmud, how to compare two stories in the Torah. We want yours in it, under your name.

**1. Try it.** In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, add a custom connector, and paste this address. There is no sign-in.

```text
https://mcp.yochai.wiki/mcp
```

Ask it what you would ask a study partner. Tell us where it is wrong, thin or off in tone.

**2. Write a skill, or bring one you have.** There are two ways in, and they lead to the same place:

- In Yochai, open Create, write your skill, and tick "Also share this skill in the Yochai plugin".
- Or open a pull request in the public repository, with one folder and one file. The steps are in [community-skills.md](./community-skills.md).

A Yochai team member reads every skill before it goes live. Your skill then appears in the plugin for every user, and in the community list in Yochai, on the same day.

**3. Keep your name on it.** Your skill carries your name and a licence you choose. We suggest CC BY 4.0, which lets others use and adapt it if they credit you. You can remove your skill at any time.

**What a good skill does**

- It teaches one method, in steps a careful student can follow.
- It quotes real passages from the library and never from memory.
- It gives sources and the range of views. It does not give rulings on Jewish law.
- It stands alone: one text file, with no attached files.

Write to us with questions at the address on the [support page](https://mcp.yochai.wiki/support).

Thank you,
The Yochai team

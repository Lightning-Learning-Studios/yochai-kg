# Disclaimer & responsible use

**This is derived study material, not a halakhic authority.** The Yochai knowledge graph is
built by parsing, embedding, and LLM-extracting structure from a corpus of Jewish texts. It is
a *chavruta* — a study partner that helps you find, connect, and explore sources — not a posek.

When you (or your agent) build on this graph:

- **Verify against the primary text.** Every passage carries a deep link to its source. Follow it.
  Translations, segmentation, and extracted structure can contain errors.
- **The concept and entity layers are machine-synthesized.** Canonical concepts, relationships,
  prerequisite ordering, and the 2.5M-node entity layer are produced by models. They are
  excellent for discovery and navigation; they are not authoritative claims about what a text
  "means" or rulings about practice.
- **Do not present output as p'sak halakha** (binding legal ruling) or as the position of any
  rabbi, community, or movement without checking the source and appropriate authorities.
- **Cite honestly.** Attribute passages to their source; distinguish primary text from your
  agent's synthesis; say when something could not be verified.
- **Respect the texts and the people who study them.** This corpus is sacred to many. Build
  things that deepen learning.

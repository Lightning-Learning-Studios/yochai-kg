# Support for Yochai: Jewish Texts

## Contact us

Write to support@lightningstudios.ai (to confirm). We answer within 2 working days (to confirm).

To report a fault in the open, use the issue list of the public repository:
https://github.com/Lightning-Learning-Studios/yochai-kg/issues

## What to include

1. The assistant you use: Claude, Claude Code or ChatGPT.
2. The question you asked.
3. What you expected, and what you got.

Do not send private information. We do not store your questions, so we cannot look them up.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. The plugin is free and needs no sign-in.

**The plugin says I reached a limit.**
The server limits requests from one network address. Wait one minute and try again.

**A passage or a translation looks wrong.**
Tell us the reference, for example "Genesis 22:2", and what is wrong. Each result has a link to the source text, so you can compare.

**Can the plugin give me a ruling on Jewish law?**
No. It shows what the sources say. For a personal decision, ask a rabbi.

**How do I remove the plugin?**
In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors or Plugins, and remove "Yochai: Jewish Texts". In ChatGPT, open Settings, then Plugins, and remove it there.

**Where can I study in more depth?**
The full Yochai study app is at https://www.yochai.wiki.

# Yochai: Jewish Texts

Study Torah, Talmud and more, inside Claude or ChatGPT. Free. No sign-in.

Yochai gives your assistant a library of more than 1,000 Jewish texts in Hebrew and English. It finds the real passage, shows its commentaries, and links you to the full text on [yochai.wiki](https://www.yochai.wiki).

## What you can ask

- "What do Jewish sources say about welcoming guests?"
- "What is this week's Torah portion, and what is one question it raises?"
- "Show me Genesis 22:2 with Rashi and Ramban."
- "Walk me through the argument on Berakhot 2a."
- "Build me a four-session course on repentance."

## Add it

This page is the address of the Yochai server. Add the plugin from the Claude or ChatGPT directory, or connect an assistant that supports the Model Context Protocol to the address `/mcp` on this site.

# Privacy Policy for Yochai: Jewish Texts

Last updated: November 20, 2026 (draft)

Yochai: Jewish Texts is a free study tool from Lightning Studios. It lets an assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT search and read Jewish texts. This page says what the server receives and what it keeps.

## The short version

- You need no account and no sign-in.
- We do not store your questions or the answers.
- We do not sell data, and we show no advertisements.

## What the server receives

When your assistant uses the plugin, it sends a request to our server. The request contains:

- the search text or the passage reference that the assistant chose;
- your network address (IP address), which every internet request carries;
- the name of the assistant software, such as "Claude" or "ChatGPT".

We do not receive your name, your email address, or the rest of your conversation.

## What the server keeps

| What we keep | Why | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A daily count of calls for each tool | To see which features people use | 24 months (to confirm) |
| A one-way hash of your network address | To apply fair-use limits and to count visitors | The hash changes every day. We cannot turn it back into an address. |
| The name of the assistant software | To count use from Claude and from ChatGPT | 24 months (to confirm) |
| Short technical logs: time, tool name, response time, error code | To find and repair faults | 30 days (to confirm) |

The server does not write your search text, your passage references, or the results to any log or database. The server holds recent results in memory for a few minutes to answer repeat requests faster. That memory holds no address and is cleared on every restart.

## Who else processes data

| Company | What it receives | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DeepInfra | The search text only, with no address and no identity | It turns the text into a list of numbers that the search uses |
| Railway | All requests, as our hosting company | It runs the server |
| Anthropic or OpenAI | Your conversation, under their own privacy policies | They run the assistant you use |

Calendar answers, such as this week's Torah portion, are computed on our server. No outside service receives them.

## Links to Yochai

Results contain links to the Yochai website. If you open a link, the privacy policy of that website applies.

## Children

The plugin is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone younger.

## Your choices

We hold no account and no stored questions, so there is nothing to export or delete for one person. To stop all processing, remove the plugin from your assistant. If you have a question about your data, write to us.

We will post changes on this page and update the date at the top.
